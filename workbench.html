PSQA8277 - MinuteGrid - MinuteGrid maps every minute from 1 to 90 in which a player has scored, and shows which players at top-5 league clubs have scored in all 90 minutes. It turns a player's goal record into a grid you can read at a glance, showing the minutes they've covered and the few they still need.
[Link↗︎ | GitHub↗︎]

  • - Covers the Premier League, LaLiga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, their domestic cups and European competitions over the last 10 years
  • - Tracks players who are one to five minutes short of the full set, so you can see who's close
  • - Updates itself every 3 hours with the latest goals

NUEX8727 - PreviouslyOn - PreviouslyOn answers "when was the last time that happened?" for European football. Pick a club, player, manager or league, build a question like "when did Arsenal last win 8 in a row?", and get the date, the match and how long it's been, along with the previous time it happened and the nearest misses. It turns every streak and scoreline into a piece of history you can look up and share.
[Link↗︎ | GitHub↗︎]

  • - Covers the top 5 leagues, their domestic cups and the Champions, Europa and Conference Leagues since 2016/17
  • - Updated every morning, with a "just happened" feed of things that hadn't been seen in over a year
  • - Every answer has its own link and preview image, ready to share
